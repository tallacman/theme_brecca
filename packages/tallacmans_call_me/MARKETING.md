# Tallacman's Call Me for Concrete CMS

**Tallacman's Call Me** adds a simple click-to-call or click-to-text link anywhere on your site — perfect for mobile visitors who want to reach you in one tap.

Built for **Concrete CMS 9**.

---

## Features

- **Call or SMS** — Choose voice call (`tel:`) or text message (`sms:`) links
- **Flexible display** — Show as a button or as a heading, span, or paragraph
- **Sanitized links** — Phone numbers are cleaned for reliable mobile dialing
- **Theme-friendly button** — Uses Bootstrap primary colors when available, with a built-in fallback style

---

## Short marketplace description

Add a click-to-call or click-to-text link with customizable display text. Show it as a button or heading. Built for Concrete CMS 9.
