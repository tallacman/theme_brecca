# Tallacman's Bodacious Buttons for Concrete CMS

**Tallacman's Bodacious Buttons** puts polished call-to-action buttons on any page—without custom code, without fighting your layout, and without sacrificing your brand.

Built for **Concrete CMS 9**, this block lets editors drop a button anywhere content needs a clear next step: a signup, a download, a contact page, or an external link. Choose your destination, set the label, tune the spacing, and decide whether the button should match your theme or carry its own look.

Every button gets a job. Every click gets a destination.

---

## Why editors love it

Most sites don't need another generic link. They need a button that *looks* like a button, sits where it belongs, and sends people somewhere on purpose. Bodacious Buttons handles the destination, the presentation, and the polish—so your team can focus on the message, not the markup.

---

## Features

### Smart linking
- **Page links** — Send visitors to any page on your site.
- **External URLs** — Link out to tools, forms, partner sites, and more. URLs work with or without `https://`.
- **File downloads** — Attach PDFs, brochures, media, and documents from the asset library.
- **Force download** — Choose whether a file opens in the browser or downloads immediately.
- **Custom button text** — Write your own label, or leave it blank and let the block use the page name, URL, or file title.
- **Open in new window** — Keep visitors on your site or send them elsewhere in a new tab.

### Layout control
- **Adjustable spacing** — Set top, right, bottom, and left padding so every button breathes in its context.
- **Works anywhere** — Blog posts, landing pages, sidebars, footers, and content areas.

### Styling that fits your site
- **Use theme styling** — Match your site's existing button design automatically. One click, consistent branding.
- **Style it yourself** — Need a button that stands out? Switch to custom styling and set your own:
  - Background color
  - Text color
  - Hover background color
  - Hover text color
  - Font family (from your theme's CMS webfonts)
  - Font weight
  - Font size

Custom styling options stay hidden until you need them—so simple edits stay simple, and power users get full control when they want it.

### Built for Concrete CMS 9
- Clean, organized edit interface with fieldsets and helpful guidance
- Validation that catches bad URLs and missing destinations before publish
- Theme-ready output that respects your site's design system

## Usage

1. Add **Tallacman's Bodacious Buttons** to your page or area.
2. Under **Button Link**, choose **Page**, **External URL**, or **File Download** and pick your destination.
3. Under **Button Style**, choose **Use theme styling** or **Style this button myself**.
4. Under **Spacing**, adjust padding so the button sits comfortably in the layout.
5. Save and publish.
